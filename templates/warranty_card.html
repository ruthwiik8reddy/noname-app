Aftercare Guidelines
  • Do not wash the vehicle for the first 7 days after service.
  • Avoid automatic car washes with brushes — use touchless or hand wash only.
  • Use a pH-neutral automotive shampoo for routine washing.
  • Do not apply wax, polish, or abrasive products over the coated surface.
  • Remove bird droppings, tree sap, and insect residue promptly — do not let them bake in sun.
  • For ceramic and graphene coatings, apply a maintenance spray every 3–4 months to maintain hydrophobic performance.
  • Park in shaded or covered areas where possible to extend coating life.
